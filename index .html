Suitability Intake
COMP-05
 Design complete; in development
Design complete; in development. Designed end-to-end AI workflow for KYC information extraction covering ~4,000 reviews/month (21,000 clients; 47,000 accounts). Phase 2 (PM-triggered) — AI validates data against compliance suitability matrix, classifies exceptions, generates draft suitability note. PM reviews via AI dashboard; approved data pushes to HubSpot. Continuous improvement loop support three types of feedback: missing calls, extraction mistakes and generation issues.
